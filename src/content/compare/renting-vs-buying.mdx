## Ignore the cultural pressure

Buying a home is not automatically the better financial move. The right answer depends on time horizon, local market conditions, maintenance risk, and how stable your cash flow is.

## Renting preserves flexibility

Renting can be stronger when:

- you may move within a few years
- your emergency fund is still thin
- the monthly cost gap favors renting

## Buying can be stronger over long horizons

Buying starts to improve when you can stay put long enough to spread closing costs, absorb repairs, and steadily build equity.

## Compare monthly life, not just theoretical wealth

If ownership leaves you house-poor, the spreadsheet advantage can turn into a real-life disadvantage.
